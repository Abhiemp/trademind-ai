# trademind-ai
an AI-powered application that analyzes historical stock market data, identifies market trends, and generates insights to help users understand potential investment opportunities.
TradeMind AI is an AI-powered stock market analysis and paper trading platform focused on the Indian stock market (NSE and BSE). It brings stock analysis, technical indicators, market insights and simulated trading together in one interface.

The platform uses an LLM-powered financial assistant to analyse market data, explain stock movements, compare companies and provide data-backed BUY, SELL or HOLD assessments. It also includes portfolio tracking and strategy backtesting to help users explore trading strategies without risking real money.

Built with React, FastAPI and Python, TradeMind AI aims to make stock market analysis more accessible through a clean interface and practical financial tools.
